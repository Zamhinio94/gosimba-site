What we get asked on every call.

What happens when a customer needs a person?
They get one. Anyone on your team can take over a live call from their phone, and Simba hands over with a summary of what's been said so far. If nobody picks up, he takes a message, books the callback into the diary, and the office sees it flagged first thing.
Can we control what he says?
Yes, and you should. Your greeting, your rates, what counts as urgent, what he's allowed to promise and what he's not. It's written down in plain English and you can change it yourself. He follows the rules you set, and only those.
What if he gets it wrong?
He will, sometimes. That's why nothing goes to a customer without a person's tap until you decide otherwise, and why every call is recorded and searchable. When he gets something wrong, you tell us, we fix the rule, and you can see the fix in the next call. Week one is for finding these.
Does he work with BigChange or Joblogic?
He sits alongside them. Bookings he makes land in your diary, and he reads the diary to offer real slots. Your system stays the system of record, and if you switch him off, nothing in it changes. Commusoft and simPRO too. If you run on a whiteboard and a mobile, he runs on his own.
Does he move jobs around without asking?
No. A reschedule is a suggestion like any other booking. He offers the caller real alternative gaps, and the move waits for a tap from your office, unless you have put reschedules on Auto for that engineer. Every move is logged with who approved it.
Will customers know they're talking to an AI?
If they ask, he says so. He introduces himself as Simba from your firm, sounds British, and doesn't pretend. Most callers with water on the floor care about the answer to "when can someone come", and he gives them one.
Where does our data go?
Your calls, transcripts and customer details are stored in the UK and EU, under your firm's name, and used to run your account. They're not used to train anything for anyone else. You can export or delete the lot.
What does it cost?
A flat monthly price for the firm. No per-seat, no per-call, no 24-month contract. We'll give you the number on the first call, and it won't change when you add engineers.
